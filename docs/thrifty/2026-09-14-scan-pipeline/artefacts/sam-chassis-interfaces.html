eQuad chassis interface geometry

For Sam · Lakeview Engineering review and fabrication recommendations requested 2026-09-15

Purpose

The scan-to-CAD fit found tube axes, radii and mounting planes across six regions of the chassis. Three issues came out of it. Short version: the model cuts off some real features, and the tube ODs it did keep are unclear — we need to rescan and measure to close both gaps. Jeremy will do this directly (the bike is disassembled), so the asks below are for your fabrication input, not verification.

What a good fit looks like, next to a poor one

Real scan points (grey), with a fitted cylinder overlaid. Green = accepted, red = rejected.

Item 1

Rear axle housing — one side was cut off by a size filter

  • Issue: the fit only accepted cylinders with radius 11–16, 17–20 or 22–26 mm. The scan shows both housings, but the left one measured 27.03 mm — 1 mm over that top bracket — so it was dropped before it was ever checked for fit quality. The right side was kept, but three separate measurements of the two sides still disagree by up to 3 mm (23.9–27.1 mm).
  • Action taken: added a 26–29 mm bracket alongside the existing ones (not replacing them, so the already-accepted right side is unaffected) and re-ran the fit. The left side got into refinement this time, but its refined rms came back at 1.94 mm — still a poor fit even against a relaxed 1.0 mm threshold. That rules out a bracket or threshold problem: the real surface there doesn't sit on a clean cylinder as well as the coarse RANSAC estimate suggested, which points to a physical feature near the housing (flange, weld, step). Rescan or calliper both sides directly.

Item 3

~13 mm tube family — not one gauge, a mix of clean and messy fits

  • Issue: across five of the six regions, 81 well-supported cylinders (over 50,000 scan points each) cluster at 12.5–15.5 mm radius. Relaxing the accept threshold from 0.8 mm to 1.0 mm let 48 of them through; 33 are still rejected, some badly (up to 2.29 mm rms). This isn't one tube gauge landing just past a threshold — it's a real, common tube size with some fits landing on clean sections and others on welds or junctions.
  • Action taken: raised the accept threshold to 1.0 mm (from 0.8 mm). Recovered 46 more accepted primitives dataset-wide (127 → 173).
  • Action: calliper tank-mounts/cyl/001 (r 13.50 mm, rms 0.223 mm, support 181,694 — the cleanest fit in the family) to confirm the tube gauge.

Rescan / measure priority

Bike is fully disassembled — each part can be pulled and rescanned or callipered on its own.

  1. Rear axle housing, both sides — highest priority. Sets the intermediate-drive mount geometry. Rescan the housing alone if possible; that also settles axis and length, and should show whether a flange or step near the housing is why the left side won't fit a clean cylinder.
  2. tank-mounts/cyl/001 — the cleanest fit in the ~13 mm tube family (rms 0.223 mm). One calliper reading here confirms the gauge for the accepted members of that family.
  3. rear-axle/cyl/017 — medium priority. Identify what this feature physically is; the automated re-fit couldn't confirm it.
  4. Front-flange dowels cyl/028, cyl/038 — lowest priority, small fasteners.
