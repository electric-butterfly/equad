## Notes
Preconditions checked: pipeline/segment.py and all six primitives-<region>.json present;
data/crops/engine-bay/engine-bay_full.ply present. `.venv-mesh` is absent in this worktree
(git-ignored, per-machine) — ran via the main checkout's interpreter,
`C:/SANDPIT/equad/.venv-mesh/Scripts/python.exe`, invoked with this worktree as the working
directory.

Canary (items 1-2, rear-axle) caught a real bug before the batch: the first cut of `fit.py` selected
full-res points by radial distance to the RANSAC cylinder's *infinite* axis only, with no bound
along the axis. On the rear-axle region (a 480 mm x 480 mm crop) that swept in unrelated points
sharing the same orientation and radius elsewhere in the crop, driving every candidate's refined rms
into the tens of millimetres (0 of 165 accepted). Fixed by also bounding the along-axis selection to
the RANSAC primitive's own `length` (plus the 3 mm margin) before refining. After the fix, 12 of 165
rear-axle candidates are accepted, rms values in the expected sub-mm to low-single-mm range.

The first deviation-render implementation used no PNG scanline filter and exceeded the WP prompt's
500000-byte cap on 3 of 6 regions (up to 730519 B after trying an Up-filter, which made it worse,
not better, since the images are sparse dots on a large flat white background that zlib already
handles well without filtering). Fixed by rendering at 1.0 mm/px instead of 0.6 mm/px; all six
renders now finish between 103182 and 239196 bytes. This is a rendering-resolution choice, not
specified numerically by plan section 4.6, so it was free to adjust to meet the WP's own byte cap.

Out-of-scope finding logged: findings/WP-06.md, on the rear-axle bracket/housing mismatch above.
