Importing the battery boxes into Fusion

Files, import order and the checks that prove the origin, axes and units arrived unchanged.

1. What the files are

Every file is in the vehicle datum frame: ISO 8855, millimetres, X forward, Y left, Z up, origin below the rear axle housing axis. No file carries a transform; each body sits at its vehicle coordinates.

FileWhat it holds
datum-marker.stepThree bars from the origin. Import this first, alone.
assembly.stepBoth battery boxes, the motor placeholder and the propeller-shaft keep-out, at their vehicle coordinates.
box.stepOne enclosure with its lid, plates and fuses, no cells.
box-full.stepOne enclosure with its 26 cells.
block.stepThe 26-cell block on its own.
cell.stepOne CALB L148N58A cell.
chassis_datum_200k.stlThe scanned frame, 200 000 faces, in the same vehicle frame. A mesh, not a solid; it lives in the cleaned scan folder, not in this folder.

2. Before you import

  1. Start an empty design and set its modelling orientation to Z up. Look for the setting named Default modeling orientation in Preferences, or the equivalent in your release.
  2. Import datum-marker.step alone, first, before anything else.

3. The origin and axis check

The three bars have different lengths on purpose (axis_x 500.0 mm, axis_y 300.0 mm, axis_z 200.0 mm). Each starts at the origin and runs in the positive direction. Read the bounding box of each bar in Fusion and compare it with this table, in Fusion's X, Y and Z.

BodyMinimum corner (mm)Maximum corner (mm)Tolerance
axis_x(0.0, 0.0, 0.0)(500.0, 20.0, 20.0)0.5 mm
axis_y(0.0, 0.0, 0.0)(20.0, 300.0, 20.0)0.5 mm
axis_z(0.0, 0.0, 0.0)(20.0, 20.0, 200.0)0.5 mm

What a failure looks like: the wrong bar longest means an axis is swapped; a bar running into negative coordinates means an axis is mirrored; sizes out by a factor of 25.4 or 1000 mean a unit error; a minimum corner away from (0.0, 0.0, 0.0) means the origin moved.

If any value fails, stop. Write down the three observed bar lengths and which Fusion axis each one lies along. Do not rotate or move anything to make it pass.

4. Import the assembly

With the check in section 3 passed, import assembly.step into a new design in the same orientation. Each body should appear under its name with its own colour; the keep-out is written with 35 % opacity. Axis-aligned bounding boxes, in vehicle coordinates:

BodyMinimum corner (mm)Maximum corner (mm)Tolerance
box_left(510.2, 15.0, 288.5)(976.2, 261.6, 620.2)0.5 mm
box_right(510.2, -261.6, 288.5)(976.2, -15.0, 620.2)0.5 mm
motor(122.6, -105.0, 485.0)(381.4, 105.0, 695.0)0.5 mm
keepout(400.0, -60.0, 280.0)(1200.0, 60.0, 400.0)0.5 mm

5. Import the chassis

Insert chassis_datum_200k.stl as a mesh body, choosing millimetres if you are asked for a unit. Expected bounding box:

BodyMinimum corner (mm)Maximum corner (mm)Tolerance
chassis_datum_200k(-311.4, -587.7, 4.3)(1663.0, 562.7, 1206.1)1.0 mm

A result 25.4 times too large or small means the wrong unit was chosen.

6. Combine and look

With the datum marker, the assembly and the chassis in one design and nothing moved, the marker's bars lie along the chassis' origin, the left box is on the +Y side, the motor is behind the boxes (smaller X), and the keep-out runs along X at Z 340 mm.

7. Rules

  • The CAD is the master. A placement or parameter change is made in cad/params.toml, the files are regenerated, and the new files are imported again. Do not edit the imported geometry and treat it as the source.
  • The committed placement has not been checked clear of the frame: no placement in the swept grid passed (sweep-report.md).
  • The plastics, foot boards, tank and seat base are not in the scan and are not in these files.
