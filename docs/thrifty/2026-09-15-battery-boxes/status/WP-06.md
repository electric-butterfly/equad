## Notes

assembly.step's four solids come back from build123d's import_step in Compound child order
(box_left, box_right, motor, keepout); viewer.py asserts this via each solid's bounding-box
centre (box_left centre.Y > 0, box_right centre.Y < 0) before using positional order, so a
future change to assembly.py's child order fails loudly instead of silently mislabelling a body.

cad/viewer.py runs under .venv-cad (build123d) per cad/README.md, but the chassis decimation
needs open3d, which only exists in .venv-mesh. viewer.py shells out to
`.venv-mesh/Scripts/python.exe` for that one step via subprocess and caches the result at
data/cad/chassis_60k.b64 (git-ignored) rather than reading two venvs into one process.

The frame-clearance section of sam-battery-boxes.html carries the sweep report's full caveat,
not just the grid header: the committed [placement] (743.2, 39.2643, 440.4765, lean 82) is not a
grid point in the WP-04 sweep, 0 of 525 swept placements pass, and the least-bad point found
(min_frame 0.56 mm, 15 580 points inside) is stated as not a fit.
